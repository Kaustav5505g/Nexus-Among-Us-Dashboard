# NEXUS Event Official Rules & Playbook

Welcome to **NEXUS: Coded Chaos & Tech Mystery**!
> **"ONE REGISTRATION = TWO EVENTS! DOUBLE THE FUN, DOUBLE THE GLORY."**

---

## 🕹️ EVENT 1: CODED CHAOS (Among Us Live)

### 🎯 Objective
- **Crewmates**: Complete all debugging & logic tasks across station rooms (Reactor, Electrical, MedBay, Navigation, Admin) before the Impostors eliminate the crew or sabotage timers expire.
- **Impostors**: Blend in, covertly sabotage station terminals, eliminate crewmates without getting caught, and frame unsuspecting players during Emergency Meetings!

### 🗺️ Ship Rooms & Tasks
1. **Electrical**: Rewire broken logic gates & fix code indentation.
2. **Reactor**: Dual-station handprint confirmation & stabilize quantum core.
3. **MedBay**: Run algorithmic diagnostic scan & DNA cipher match.
4. **Navigation**: Calibrate trajectory coordinates & graph traversal puzzles.
5. **Admin**: Swipe ID card with exact swipe speed & track room occupancy.

### 🚨 Sabotage Protocol
- When an Impostor triggers a Sabotage (e.g., **Reactor Meltdown** or **O2 Depletion**), a **60-second countdown** begins on the central dashboard.
- If not resolved at the respective station within 60s, Impostors automatically win!
- **Emergency Button**: Any crewmate can call ONE Emergency Meeting per round. A 90-second debate & voting countdown commences on the dashboard.

## 🕵️‍♀️ EVENT 2: TECH MYSTERY (Detective Room)

### 🎯 Objective
- A high-profile cyber breach has compromised the NEXUS mainframe.
- Teams act as digital forensic detectives examining 5 classified case files.
- Each solved riddle, decrypted cipher, and decoded audio/hex dump yields an evidence fragment.
- The squad that stitches the evidence and reveals the perpetrator with the fastest time and highest deduction accuracy wins the Grand Mystery Trophy!

### 🏆 Scoring Formula
```
Total Score = (Tasks Completed × 100)
            + (Mystery Clues Cracked × 250)
            + (Sabotage Defusal Bonus × 150)
            - (Penalties / False Accusations × 50)
```
