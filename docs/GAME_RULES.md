# NEXUS Event Official Rules & Playbook

Welcome to **NEXUS: Coded Chaos & Tech Mystery**!  
> **"ONE REGISTRATION = TWO EVENTS! DOUBLE THE FUN, DOUBLE THE GLORY."**

---

## 🕹️ EVENT 1: CODED CHAOS (Among Us Live)

### 🎯 Objective
- **Crewmates**: Complete station challenges across physical sector rooms (Room 1, Room 2, Reactor, Electrical, Cafeteria, Navigation) before the Impostors freeze the crew or sabotage timers expire.
- **Impostors**: Blend in, deploy hostile powers from your 3 Power Ports, freeze and isolate crewmate squads, and frame unsuspecting players during Emergency Meetings!

### 🗺️ Sector Rooms & Allocation
- Teams and players are deployed into designated physical sector rooms grouped into operational zones.
- In each room, exactly **1 squad is covertly designated as the Impostor squad** (via cryptographic roll or Admin selection). All other squads in the room act as Crewmates.

### ⚡ Impostor 3-Port Power Mechanics
Every Impostor team has **3 Power Ports** accessible from their station terminal:
1. **Sabotage Lights** (Room Disruption): Plunges the sector into darkness. 30s Cooldown, 20s Duration.
2. **Terminal Freeze** (Targeted Strike): Freezes the terminal of a specific target Crewmate team in the same room, locking their actions for 30 seconds.
3. **Comms Blackout** (Targeted Strike): Jam radio signals and forensic decryptors for the selected Crewmate squad.
4. **Additional Arsenal**: Door Lockdown, Fake Clue Inject, and Radio Jammer configurable by station facilitators.

### ⚠️ Crewmate Hostile Effects
- When targeted by an Impostor power, the Crewmate team's terminal triggers a pulsating alert:
  > **"⚠️ HOSTILE IMPOSTOR POWER ACTIVE — Action Locked (00:XX Remaining)"**
- Crewmates must wait out the countdown timer before resuming station tasks or decryption attempts.

### 🚨 Sabotage & Emergency Protocol
- **Sabotages**: When critical station sabotages trigger, countdown sirens alert all sectors. Failure to defuse before expiration results in an Impostor victory.
- **Emergency Button**: Any crewmate squad can call an Emergency Meeting to summon station coordinators and vote on suspect squads.

## 🕵️‍♀️ EVENT 2: TECH MYSTERY (Detective Room)

### 🎯 Objective
- A high-profile cyber breach has compromised the NEXUS mainframe.
- Teams act as digital forensic detectives examining classified case files and clues across sector rooms.
- Each solved riddle, decrypted cipher, and decoded audio/hex dump yields an evidence fragment.
- The squad that stitches the evidence and reveals the perpetrator with the fastest time and highest deduction accuracy wins the Grand Mystery Trophy!

---

## 🏆 Scoring Formula

```
Total Score = (Tasks Completed × 100)
            + (Mystery Clues Cracked × 250)
            + (Sabotage Defusal Bonus × 150)
            - (Penalties / False Accusations × 50)
```
